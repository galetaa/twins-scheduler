# AI-Use Disclosure Draft

An OpenAI language model provided substantial assistance beyond copyediting and ordinary internet search. The assistance included organizing the research specification, generating candidate formalizations and counterexamples for human verification, locating and classifying potentially relevant public sources, drafting and restructuring prose, producing and revising research code and artifact scripts, generating tables and figures from versioned evidence, and checking consistency among claims, source files, and reported results.

The model is not an author. Human authors are responsible for independently verifying every theorem statement, proof, citation, source characterization, experimental result, program, license, security interpretation, and novelty claim before submission. Human authors must also ensure that the manuscript is original, that citations accurately represent the sources, that no confidential information was supplied to the model, and that all use complies with the final venue policy.

This disclosure should be retained or adapted whenever the assistance described above exceeds the final venue’s threshold for disclosure.
