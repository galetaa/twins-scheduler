This ledger follows the revised manuscript and appendices in
`manuscript/markdown/`. Current numbering is **Theorem 1: legal relational
preservation; Theorem 2: behavioral quorum-pivot separation; Theorem 3:
repair-frontier characterization; Corollary 1: minimum phase budget for unit weights**. The

paper theorem numbers.

## Evidence interpretation and validation status

The revision distinguishes a paper proof, a Lean theorem over its stated
hypotheses, a finite executable witness/enumeration, and primary-source
identity checks. These evidence types cannot substitute for each other.

- The new arbitrary-contract result is in
  `SchedulerClosure.RepairFrontier`. Legal scheduler realization and
  discharge of its behavioral/observation hypotheses remain obligations for
  any protocol instance.
- Graph reversal, graph-to-execution correspondence, and optimal graph
  capacity are **paper proofs with finite cross-checks**. Lean checks the
  arithmetic after reusable capacity is supplied, not those graph claims.
- The old 384 Python/Go rows per implementation concern the old Boolean
  terminal classifier. Neither those rows nor
  `bounded_tendermint_refines_quorum_pivot` prove transition refinement.
  Legacy filenames and report fields containing “refinement” are preserved
  as historical identifiers.
- The new concrete model has **12 cases and 14 dedicated tests**. It has no
  independent Go implementation or Lean transition proof. Direct late
  precommit controls are non-FIFO; the separately declared relay controls
  pass the admitted-vote FIFO ordering audit.

**Scientific validation gate: PASS.** The integrated `scripts/test_all.sh`
and `scripts/build_formal.sh` runs completed successfully: 35 Python tests,
Go tests, 8 positive SMT obligations returning UNSAT, 4 negative controls
returning SAT, Lean 4.33.0 build (12 jobs), the selected 20-declaration axiom
audit, source hygiene, and independent `leanchecker SchedulerClosure`
rechecking (exit 0). SMT used the pinned `z3-solver` 4.13.3.0 distribution.
The new `results/generated/lean_verification.json` receipt records the
successful commands and SHA-256 hashes of the Lean source files;
`assemble_formal_report.py` rejects missing, unsuccessful, or source-stale
receipts. The refreshed evidence is distinct from retained historical PASS
reports. Typesetting and PDF inspection are separate from this scientific
gate.

Row statuses identify the kind of evidence that passed. In particular,
`PAPER_GRAPH_FINITE_PASS_LEAN_ARITHMETIC_PASS` does not mean the graph theorem
was kernel-checked: the graph proof remains on paper, the stated finite
instances were executed, and only the separate capacity arithmetic was
mechanized. Likewise, `PAPER_PROOF_ONLY` denotes an unmechanized paper proof,
not an assertion of a missing executable test. A successful build does not
enlarge any declaration's formal scope.
